Rotating ticker: position counter style

Each style shows four frames of one rotation through "bar": ["crypto:bitcoin", "portfolio", "VOO", "VTSAX"]. On equity frames the market is closed (Saturday), so they get option D's treatment: dimmed, with a sleep icon. The counter is a number, so it works with 3 items or 30. Pick a counter style.
